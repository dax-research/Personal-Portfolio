## ℹ️ About

I am a BTech Computer Engineering student at CHARUSAT with a strong passion for Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. This portfolio is a reflection of my academic and professional milestones, designed to provide a comprehensive view of my capabilities and interests.

## ✨ Features

- **Hero Section**: Dynamic introduction with particle network animation and typing effect.
- **About Me**: Brief bio and professional summary.
- **Experience**: Timeline of my professional roles and internships.
- **Education**: Academic background and achievements.
- **Projects**: Showcase of my key projects with links to GitHub repositories.
- **Skills**: Visual representation of my technical skills (Frontend, Backend, AI/ML, Tools).
- **Awards & Achievements**: Highlight of hackathon wins and other recognitions.
- **Certifications**: Gallery of professional certifications.
- **Contact**: Simple contact form and social media links.
- **Responsive Design**: Fully optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- **Interactive UI**: Hover effects, smooth scrolling, and custom cursor for an engaging user experience.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **HTML5**: For semantic structure.
- **CSS3**: For styling, animations, and responsive layout.
- **JavaScript (Vanilla)**: For interactivity, animations (particle network, typing effect), and DOM manipulation.
- **Font Awesome**: For icons.
- **Google Fonts**: Typography (Inter, Space Mono).
